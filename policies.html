Our Service Policies

Please take a quick moment to look over how we handle pricing, supplies, and bookings so everything goes smoothly on cleaning day.

📋 Pricing & Estimates Policy

Our online quote calculator provides a baseline estimate based on standard cleaning needs, not a final contract price. Before we start, we will perform a quick physical walkthrough of your space to confirm the final amount. Factors that can increase the price include heavy pet hair buildup, dried grease or oil, extreme clutter, general neglect, or heavy post-construction dust film.

🧼 Supplies & Equipment

We arrive fully prepared with our own cleaning supplies, including our sprays, cleaning cloths, brooms, and mops. If you prefer us to use your own vacuum cleaner or specific brands of cleaning products instead, just let us know before we arrive.

🚗 Vehicle & Interior Care

For our Car Wash & Interior Detailing packages, please make sure all personal items and loose valuables are completely removed from the inside of your car before our scheduled arrival window.

🚫 Booking Limitations & Exclusions

To keep our team safe, we do not accept cleaning jobs that involve hazardous conditions. We cannot clean spaces with:

  • Severe biohazard messes or biological fluids.
  • Extreme hoarder-level conditions or unsafe areas.
  • Active heavy mold breakouts or severe pest issues.

Need clarification on a policy?

Send us a quick text or email! We are happy to clarify details surrounding scheduling and service parameters.
